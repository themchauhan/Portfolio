Try at home together (10 minutes)

  1. Ask your child: “What is AI?” and “How did you make the computer learn rock, paper, scissors?”
  2. Play Quick Draw together at quickdraw.withgoogle.com. No sign-up is needed.
  3. Ask them to tell you the 5 golden rules on the right.

5 golden rules of AI

  1. Never share your name, address, phone or photos with AI
  2. Check important facts before you believe them
  3. Use AI to learn and practise, not to copy homework
  4. Ask a parent or teacher before using a new AI app
  5. Be kind: no fake pictures or mean content about anyone
