How the program works

How the program works

3 levels, step by step

Every student starts at Level 1 (Basics) and moves to Level 2 (Builder) and Level 3 (Advanced). Students with prior experience can start higher after the demo.

8 weeks per level

One 2-hour class every weekend (16 hours per level), with a short fun task between classes.

A project every level

Each level ends with a showcase where students present what they built to parents.

Certificate

Students receive a certificate of completion for every level they finish.
