How the classes run

Every weekend

Saturday: learn a new idea and build it together. Sunday: build your own version, then show and tell. A small task between weekends.

Every level

8 weeks, 16 one-hour classes. Ends with an online showcase for parents.

Small batches

10–15 students, live online on Google Meet.

Where to start

Everyone starts at Level 1. Students with prior experience can start higher after the free demo.

What students need

Classes are live online on Google Meet. Level 1 works on a phone or computer; a laptop or computer is needed for Levels 2 and 3. No coding knowledge is needed to start.

Takes home: A live website or app and a project they can show colleges.

Safety and honest use

  • Students use AI chatbots only with parent permission and never share personal details.
  • Every AI answer is checked against NCERT, teachers or trusted sources.
  • AI is for understanding and practice, never for copying homework or cheating in exams.
  • Every level covers privacy, deepfakes and scams.
